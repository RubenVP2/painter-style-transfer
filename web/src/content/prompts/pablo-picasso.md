# Pablo Picasso — Cubisme

> ⚠️ Le cubisme réinterprète la géométrie par nature : le bloc PRESERVE garantit ici la reconnaissabilité du personnage plutôt que sa géométrie exacte.

```
Using the provided image as the strict base, transform it into a
Pablo Picasso cubist painting.

FORMAT (output canvas):
- Vertical 9:16 aspect ratio, 1080x1920 pixels, optimized for TikTok
- Reframe or extend the scene to fill the vertical canvas naturally
  (extend the background with muted cubist planes —
  no cropping of the character, no black bars, no blurred fill)
- Keep the character and key elements centered in the middle of the
  frame: clear of the top 250px, bottom 700px and right 240px
  (TikTok UI overlay zones)

PRESERVE (identity must remain readable):
- The character must stay clearly recognizable: same identity,
  hairstyle, outfit and pose readable through the cubist
  fragmentation
- The scene structure and element hierarchy

APPLY (style layer only):
- Geometric fragmentation into angular planes and facets
- Multiple viewpoints shown simultaneously (profile and front view
  merged in one figure)
- Muted analytical-cubist palette: ochre, terracotta, warm browns,
  greys, with a few bold accent colors
- Flat matte gouache-like planes with sharp edges

CONSTRAINTS:
- Cubist reinterpretation applies to forms and perspective, but the
  character's identity and recognizability stay intact
- No added elements, no text, no signature, no watermark, no border
```
