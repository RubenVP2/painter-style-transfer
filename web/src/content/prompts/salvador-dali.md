# Salvador Dalí — Surréalisme

> ⚠️ Le surréalisme transforme l'environnement par nature : la distorsion onirique s'applique au décor, jamais à l'identité du personnage.

```
Using the provided image as the strict base, transform it into a
Salvador Dalí surrealist oil painting.

FORMAT (output canvas):
- Vertical 9:16 aspect ratio, 1080x1920 pixels, optimized for TikTok
- Reframe or extend the scene to fill the vertical canvas naturally
  (extend the background with a vast dreamlike desert under a deep
  blue sky — no cropping of the character, no black bars, no
  blurred fill)
- Keep the character and key elements centered in the middle of the
  frame: clear of the top 250px, bottom 700px and right 240px
  (TikTok UI overlay zones)

PRESERVE (identity must remain intact):
- The character must stay clearly recognizable: same face identity,
  hairstyle, outfit and pose — rendered with precise, realistic
  detail
- The character remains the stable anchor of the scene

APPLY (style layer only):
- Hyper-precise, polished oil rendering of a dreamlike scene
- Surrealist environment: melting forms, floating objects, impossible
  perspectives, long sharp shadows
- Warm Catalan palette: desert ochres, deep blue skies, crisp
  twilight light
- Uncanny, dream-logic atmosphere reminiscent of
  "The Persistence of Memory"

CONSTRAINTS:
- Surreal distortion applies to the environment, never to the
  character's identity, face or pose
- No added elements on the character, no text, no signature,
  no watermark, no border
```
